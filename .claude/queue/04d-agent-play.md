`bun scripts/play.js --seed N --seat A1 [--seat B2 …]`: a headless, turn-based match. The sim pauses at decision points (every 0.5 s of game time, sooner on events: damage taken, Ball spawn, cooldown ready, death, respawn) and prints a compact text observation (own HP, cooldowns, level; nearby units with distance and bearing; visible enemy windups; structures; the Ball; the clock). It reads one JSON line per seat: move, attack <id>, cast Q|W|E at x,y, stop, pickup, throw at x,y, wait <s>. Seats mix agents, bots and idle. Actions become ordinary intent frames, so play stays deterministic and identical to a human's. Every match writes a replay file the browser plays back at `?mode=moba&replay=<file>`. Observations only show what the seat could see (same honesty as the bots' delayed perception). Tests: a scripted agent through stdin wins against idle; replay reproduces the match tick for tick; observation stays under ~1 KB.
