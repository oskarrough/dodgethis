Mandador, the whole kit

To review · 2026-10-29

Does each action read as a coil, a release and a mark, and do the support actions land as hard as the damage?

One clip, seven steps, at the match camera on Overthrow: the tennis ball, Pass at an enemy (hit and slow), Pass to an ally (the catch and the heal), Pass thrown wide, Tag out, Rally ring, and the trait, where Mandador falls and her cutout throws from the touchline and takes two seconds off her respawn. Every step is driven by a real pointer, real Q W E key events and right or left clicks, and each is asserted from the facts the sim produced (see proof.json).

Cast points are unchanged (0.3 s for the ball, Pass, Tag out and Rally ring; 0.35 s for the cutout throw). The ball hit at 0.55 s, Pass at 0.53 s. What changed is the body (each cast coils over its cast point and snaps on release, which the old poses did not do, and the basic had no pose at all), the marks the release and the landing leave, and the status marks (guard, slow, haste) that did not exist.

Frame-stepped: the sim advanced exactly 1/60 s per tick whatever the load, two ticks to a frame, so the clip plays at authored speed (1x). It is one hero and a staged row, not a crowded fight, and it has no HUD. It measures nothing about FPS or input latency. Wall time was a few minutes on a shared, software-rendered machine.
