Juice shortlist

Nine ways a blow could land, each drawn in our own flat ink, looping on a stand-in fight: blue strikes red. They are sketches in plain three.js, not the game, to choose which ones we build into the shared kit. Every one is cheap enough for our WebGL style pass; none needs WebGPU.

Three of the same blows done the way the three.js TSL showcases do them: additive light, soft falloff, sparks. Each sits beside its ink cousin above, 3, 4 and 7.
