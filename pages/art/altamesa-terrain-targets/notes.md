# Altamesa terrain

The close take makes the road legible. The bold take gives its red stone more weight and lets sand cross the paving; that is the build target. The generated banners are excluded because scenery must not wear team red.

Build order: warm light and clearer haze; carved stone; fewer, larger rim remnants; distant mesas; then a continuous worn floor with sand at its margins. The shade wing takes lilac slabs, the sun wing warm clay, Tagoror a square council pavement and Gnomon a long, bordered approach. Standing remnants stay inside existing cover or beyond the walking bounds.

The target images are review references, not shipping textures. Both were generated with the built-in image tool using the stored prompts and the ordered references listed beside them.

The sun follows recorded Warden victory ticks, easing for six seconds after each fall. Eight updates per second leave the existing 80 ms shadow-print debounce time to redraw during the transition; updating it every frame would postpone shadows until the sun stopped.

Validation: the unit suite and map smoke passed (271 passed, one skipped). An isolated guest snapshot check, with unsupported dungeon units omitted, confirmed identical sun progress and rejection of future victory ticks; end-to-end guest play awaits dungeon networking.
