Create a production ground-decal atlas, exactly 2048 x 2048 pixels. Exactly FOUR columns and FOUR rows, 16 equally sized 512 x 512 cells. One separate centred decal in each cell; invisible grid, NO grid lines, no dividers, no labels, no numbers, no text. Row-major order, left to right then top to bottom, exactly as the list below. Each decal occupies about 360–430 px of its cell, leaving at least 40 px of untouched magenta gutter on all sides. Do not merge neighbouring cells.

Background must be uniform PURE MAGENTA RGB(255,0,255), #ff00ff. This is a chroma-key backing, not transparent. Absolutely no magenta or pink-purple key colour inside any decal. Do not shade, vignette or texture the magenta. Each decal has an irregular sparse silhouette, broken fading wash edges, never a square backing plate or hard rectangular crop. Do not spread a floor across the sheet. Objects may contain stone locally where appropriate, but each is an isolated mark that can be scattered over existing flagstones.

Seen straight down, orthographic, flat even light; no perspective, no visible vertical sides, no cast shadows, no directional highlights, no rendered depth, no 3D product render. Drawn flat colour with a subtle soft wash, matching the line and broad quiet planes of flags-drawn.webp. Sparse fine navy #26445f strokes only where truly needed; most cracks and outlines are soft medium-value local stone colour. No large dark regions, black, strong contrast, bright highlights, red, gold, text, UI, characters, units or ornamental borders. Avoid noisy grain, excessive tiny fragments, photographic textures, glossy surfaces, neon and strong glow. These must read as humble worn marks on a fighting floor, each readable at 128 px but quieter than a unit or telegraph.
