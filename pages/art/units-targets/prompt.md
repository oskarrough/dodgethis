Ibis: slender sea-teal hide and worn cloth capsule, three reed arrows with team-blue fletchings tilted back, ink bow. An angular short ivory beak and folded brow contained within the original capsule envelope; small eyes, no separate large bird head. The arrows remain the longest silhouette feature.
Kappa: low wide terracotta hide shell, rounded square roof, neckless. Give the roof a few broad cut shell planes and worn corners. A brow cut into the forward edge, tiny eyes underneath. Large cream hide glove preserves its thumb gap and finger bumps, squared fingertips, two broad seams. Team blue belt only.
Carambola: preserve existing forward triangular cream cloth body and faceted stone head, short hard beak, small angular ears and worn open wooden paddle on the side, rolled net on back. Tiny eyes under the brow. Team blue waist band only. No blue paddle.
Mandador: a flat upright painted wooden board, 1.3 m wide and 0.25 m deep from above, NOT a humanoid or bird. Chipped squared top edge, two broad wood seams and a stern painted brow in the forward face. Same small hand and forward flared speaking-trumpet. Team blue on the trumpet only.
Lasso: an open terracotta crescent of weathered hide, KEEP the large empty crescent centre and gap. Existing hooked ends become chiselled and notched; small eyes cut beneath a brow at one existing end. No added head or shoulders, no filling the crescent. Thin ink line to the existing weighted yo-yo, only the yo-yo is blue.
Oolong: wide squat hexagonal driftwood drum, no separate head, worn bark side planes and a folded cloth top. Heavy bevelled brow at its front edge, small eyes. Same cream enamel tea jug on the back. Team blue sash only. Do not turn him gold or into a barrel-sized humanoid.
Undine: squat round sea-teal enamel jug body, with existing loop handle and spout. Chip the enamel at the pale lip and give the spout a hard angled end. Small eyes beneath an incised brow, no new head, no new limbs. Team blue disc only, body has NO team accent.
Salvia: preserve the SAME thin board/bar envelope and forward flared trumpet as today's source, pale bark and worn cream cloth, a shallow sage satchel relief held inside the silhouette, one notched upper corner and small eyes under a low brow. This is a surface treatment, not a new humanoid herbalist. Team blue on the trumpet only.

Take from ref-beast: creatures with dignity rather than cuteness, their weight and self-possession, NOT its glowing eyes or blue colour. From George Barr's Quiet Muse: 'the creature: funny, dignified and well dressed', NOT jewellery or gold. From the approved Altamesa target: exact low-poly construction, ink thickness and restraint. As the reference shelf says: 'creatures with dignity rather than cuteness'. Keep the funny little primitives; make them look worn and capable. No plush, no giant heads, no button eyes, no glossy eyes, no realistic fur, no shiny metal, no added weapons or horns outside the existing bounds.
