# Ibis and Carambola

Ibis follows `pages/art/concept-trial/ibis/ibis.webp`: a pale angular head above a sea-teal coat, a long hooked navy beak, ragged pale wing edges and a worn quiver rim. Her three arrows fan farther apart and carry larger team fletchings. The red scarf and metal ornaments were left behind; neither would help identify her at match size.

Carambola has two generated studies, each with its world-built prompt beside it. Study A was chosen for its broad stone wedge, low brow and triangular cream tunic. Study B has handsome dark stone, but its crown and massive arms weaken the forward triangle. The build takes A's low head, chipped cap and compact shoulders, with a navy beak for contrast against the pale court. His wooden paddle stays open at his side; its ink rim now leaves the chipped bite open too. His band is the only costume team accent.

Both costumes remain code-built geometry. The exported rigs, animation timing, hero-select dressing entry points, collision bodies and team-disc construction are unchanged. No generated image is a shipping sprite.
