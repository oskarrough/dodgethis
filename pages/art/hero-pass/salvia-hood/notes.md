# Salvia's hood, Ibis's bill and Lasso's spool

The first pass left Salvia as a pale lump with a green flap slumped over one shoulder; at the match camera nobody could have named her. Her hood is now a tall sage cone that covers her whole crown and tips forward over the brow, so from above she reads as a point over a cream hem, and stays apart from the rest of the cast in grayscale.

Ibis's bill is shorter and droops, so it no longer cuts through her health bar when she faces away. Lasso's spool is a little larger to give the team colour more room; it is still the palest team accent in the cast.
