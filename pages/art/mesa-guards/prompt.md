# sheet: A sheet of the four servants who guard Altamesa for its Wardens, made of the same clay, stone, wood and cloth as their masters (Manos is a wall of red clay hands, Fornax a terracotta kiln, Barquero a violet-robed ferryman with a driftwood pole). Image 1 is the game at the match camera: the two pale blobs by the hero are today's guards, which is the size and angle these must read at. Lay the sheet out as four columns on cream paper, one servant each, with three views per column: from above at the 58-degree match camera, from the front, and a small grayscale thumbnail at match size (about 60 px tall) to prove the silhouette. Each servant has its own shape from above and one prop, and no two share either. (1) The melee guard: a squat terracotta jar with a chipped rim worn as a crown, a dark brow band with two small cream eyes just under the rim, stubby clay feet, holding a big curved potsherd before it as a shield; from above, a round rim with a crescent shard in front. (2) The ranged guard: a slim pale-sandstone figure like a standing stone, wrapped in a hood and sash of worn violet enamel cloth, carrying a tall forked driftwood staff with a round stone cradled in the fork; from above, a narrow oval with a long diagonal staff and a dot at its end. (3) The brute: a broad, bottom-heavy clay and stone figure that carries a great chipped stone lintel across its shoulders like a yoke, its small head sunk under it, heavy clay fists; from above, a wide flat bar across a round body, a T. (4) The bardino, Barquero's dog: a lean sand-coloured clay herding dog, low and long, long snout, pointed notched ears, ribs as carved grooves, a cracked terracotta collar; from above, a long narrow wedge, nose forward, unlike any upright figure. These are neutral monsters: no team red, no team blue and no gold anywhere, not even a trim. Every one has a hard angle, small eyes under a brow, worn chipped edges, a planted stance, and a thick navy ink outline. No text or labels on the sheet.
