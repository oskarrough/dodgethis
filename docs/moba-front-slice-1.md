# MOBA front slice 1 verification

`?mode=moba` opens the mode screen. Practice cuts directly into the existing MOBA match; Back, Esc and pad B return to the dodgeball hub. Hero select, loading and post-match remain outside this slice. The implementation is in [front/](../src/plugins/moba/front/); the requirements are in [moba-front.md](moba-front.md).

The menu has four composited, undistorted SVG planes, each bounded to 110% of the viewport, with pixel-sized hatching. A signboard shares the ridge projection and movement. Pointer parallax eases in on-demand animation frames and settles to idle. Two stacked skies crossfade by opacity. Parallax holds still during the crossfade and eases towards the latest pointer afterwards; reversing a fade starts from its live opacity. Reduced motion disables both motions. Model-free screens gate WebGL draws without stopping input polling. Run-scoped style presets restore on disposal.

Acceptance was driven against the production preview with synthetic keyboard events and a mocked Gamepad API, plus browser mouse clicks. All three devices started Practice and independently backed out to the hub. A held pad confirm across the cut left the hero with no attack or cast. Practice was pad-focused in the revision screenshots at 1440 × 900, 2560 × 1080 and 390 × 844. The planet's projected horizontal and vertical scale matched at all three sizes. Prompts are separate spans, so they retain their spacing on the phone.

The idle recording contains a full 10-second hold: zero Layout, Paint, UpdateLayoutTree or RasterTask events and zero WebGL draw calls. A warmed pointer sweep at 2560 × 1440 and actual browser DPR 2 likewise had zero layouts, paints, rasters and draw calls. The fixed dodgeball court-and-players frame was byte-identical after a thin-line, hatch and alpha preset-and-restore round trip; the preset itself changed the frame and cleared sky alpha.

Revision artifacts are in BB thread storage for `thr_2vvwz379bt`, under `front-revision/`: the three screenshots, `pointer-sweep-dpr2.json`, `idle.json` and `results.json`. `rev-style-proof.json` is alongside that directory. The repo owns both browser drivers: [verify-moba-front.mjs](../scripts/verify-moba-front.mjs) runs against a production preview and takes an artifact directory; [verify-style-preset.mjs](../scripts/verify-style-preset.mjs) runs against Vite. The unit checks cover projection, frame-rate-independent easing, scoped render gating, preset validation/restoration, shared focus, Tab-only navigation, pad back edges and wind cleanup with live volume changes. The `front` tune folder registers once; its timing, motion and sounds apply live. Core music names the ambient scene `wind`.

Josefin Sans light and regular are static Latin WOFF2 subsets, preloaded from [public/fonts/](../public/fonts/), with the upstream OFL included. Darumadrop remains the display font. `bun run check` checks formatting without rewriting the checkout.
