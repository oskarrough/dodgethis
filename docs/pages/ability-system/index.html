1. Today's vocabulary, as built

An ability is a manifest object plus live numbers from its tune file: defineAbility spreads the manifest and adds a stats getter on tune[id] (src/plugins/moba/ability.js:8). A hero folder names a basic and up to four slots, which heroFromManifest resolves to ability objects (heroes.js:12, slots at heroes.js:7). Per-hero and per-ability bookkeeping lives in abilityState, built from every owner's state() (heroes.js:101).

  1. Kinds. Release branches on ability.kind: stance (casting.js:173), channel (casting.js:184), instant (casting.js:197), dash (casting.js:199), zone (casting.js:207), and everything else falls through to a shot (casting.js:222). Basics add melee (combat.js:127) and homing (abilities/tennisBall/tennisBall.js:5). Bots find their tools by kind too: first shot, zone, dash, catching stance (fight-bots.js:57-63).
  2. Ability hooks. onStart, onTick, onRelease, onEnd, onCancel (casting.js:295-369); watch every tick for every slotted ability (sim.js:309); onDashEnd (sim.js:347); onHit twice, once before damage where returning true swallows the hit (combat.js:191) and once after a non-lethal hit (combat.js:277); ready (casting.js:64), targetUnit (casting.js:112), projectileStats (casting.js:232), deferDamage for a defersDamage stance (combat.js:231), blinkTo for bots (fight-bots.js:60).
  3. Hero hooks. traits.onTick (sim.js:307), onHit, onDeath, onCatch, and traits.damageFactor (combat.js:226); bot and botDead (bots.js:237, bots.js:258); outOfPlay for Coach's cutout (sim.js:171, hud.js:80, index.js:473-589); trait() for the card.
  4. Primitives a folder can call. sim.hit, sim.displace, sim.enemiesOf (exported at sim.js:388-390); launchShot, openCatch, throwCaught, swapHero; the zones, boards and cutouts lists. And, in practice, direct writes to unit fields: Red light sets freezeUntil (abilities/redLight/redLight.js:20), Tag out cleanses by zeroing stunUntil and slow (abilities/tagOut/tagOut.js:58-59), Swig pushes into hero.shields (abilities/swig/swig.js:20).
  5. Manifest fields the engine reads. Identity and look: name, icon, card.summary/notes, effects (cue names for cast, projectile, hit, impact, pose), tell (line, cone, ring, selfCircle, tether), held (line, cone, circle, selfCircle, arrow), projectileShape (view.js:118). Behaviour flags: pierce, heal, heroOnly, catchable, aimAssist, returnsPocket, catchesShots, placesCover, root, cancelOnMove, cancelOnCast, defersDamage. Bot hints: botCone, botRadial (fight-bots.js:315-320). Stats from tune: cooldown, castPoint, range, radius, speed, damage, duration, slow, bounce, healAmount, delay, time, speedFactor, angle, sound.
  6. Unit states, each its own field. stunUntil, freezeUntil, proneUntil, slow (one slot, { until, factor }), shields (a list), displacement, shove, stance, catchWindow. Each is reset by hand on respawn (combat.js:61-81), on bench (combat.js:307-318) and on hero swap (sim-heroes.js:166-181), copied by hand into the snapshot (sim-snapshot.js:92-119) and both replicas (lane-replica.js:19-34, lane-replica.js:84-91), and checked by hand wherever it gates something (casting.js:360, control.js:114, orders.js:163, projectiles.js:328, projectiles.js:393). Engine files reading each: stunUntil 11, freezeUntil 10, proneUntil 9, slow 7, displacement 7, shields 6.
  7. Rules baked in, not chosen. The newest slow overwrites the old one, even a weaker one (combat.js:248, zones at projectiles.js:62). Speed-ups don't exist: slowFactor clamps to at most 1 (ability.js:67). A zone's landing is Rain's landing: damage and slow in a radius, no hook (projectiles.js:44-70). Damage reduction exists only per hero (traits.damageFactor), so Tag out's reduction had to live in Coach's hero file (heroes/coach/coach.js:25).

Score: 12 of 40 today, 21 after slice 1, 36 after all five slices. The four left need carried units (Snowball, Dropship, Gorge) or crowd-control immunity (Black Shield). Every "yes" today is a dash, a drag, a blink, a channel or a direct write to stunUntil; every save but the plain shield is a "no". The cheapest thing that moves the most saves is the status list.

What the five data models agree on: an ability is activation and targeting; what it does is a list of small effects (damage, heal, apply, remove, move, summon, search an area); and the lasting part is one thing on the target with a duration, tags, stacks and modifiers. WoW calls it an aura, Dota a modifier, HotS a behavior, GAS a gameplay effect with granted tags. We have the first two layers and are missing the third.

4. The proposal, smallest first

  1. Statuses on units (slice 1, then 2). Every unit gets statuses: a plain list. A folder declares its statuses next to its hooks and applies them with sim.apply(unit, 'tangle.slow', { source }). The engine knows a short list of things a status can carry, and asks the list, never the ability:
    • Modifiers: moveSpeed (below 1 slows, above 1 hastes), damageTaken, damageDealt.
    • absorb: an amount that eats damage before HP (today's shields).
    • periodic: damage or heal every N seconds, sourced to the applier.
    • Hooks: onApply, onExpire, onRemove, onDamageTaken (return what's left; Stagger, Shallow Grave, Borrowed Time).
    • States, from slice 2: stunned, frozen and prone, read through one gate table (blocked(unit, verb)), so a state's meaning lives in one place. rooted, silenced and untargetable are rows added with the first folder that needs them. Displacement becomes a core.motion status carrying its path (from, to, ticks) on the host only.
    • Bookkeeping: tags (debuff, slow, shield…), stack (replace, refresh, independent, stacks with a max), bar (shield or pending, for the health bar), icon. 
    // abilities/tangle/tangle.js
statuses: {
	slow: { tags: ['debuff', 'slow'], stack: 'independent',
		moveSpeed: ({ stats }) => 1 - stats.slow, duration: ({ stats }) => stats.duration },
},
// in onRelease: sim.hit({ ...shot, applies: ['tangle.slow'] }, target)

// abilities/swig/swig.js
statuses: { shield: { tags: ['shield'], stack: 'independent', absorb: true, bar: 'shield' } },
// in onTick: sim.apply(hero, 'swig.shield', { amount: stats.shieldPerSecond * dt, duration: stats.shieldLife })
    The name is statuses because effects already means cue names (ability.js:32).
  2. Interaction rules.
    • Stuns land in full: no diminishing returns, no immunity window. A second stun takes the later end time; durations never sum.
    • Stunned blocks moving, casting, attacking and channels. Rooted blocks moving and dashes, not casting. Silenced blocks slots, not moving or the basic. Frozen and prone are stuns with their own pose.
    • One motion at a time; the newest replaces the old, as today. A motion stops control and cancels casts, channels and dashes, as displace does now.
    • Slows: the strongest active slow wins (the lowest movement factor); they do not multiply. Each application keeps its own expiry, even from the same source. A weaker slow takes over if it outlasts the stronger one. A slow cleanse removes them all. Hastes multiply with each other and with the slow.
    • Absorbs drain in the order they were applied (today's order), after deferral and before HP. Periodic damage is absorbed too (Stagger's card promises this).
    • Cleanse removes statuses tagged debuff from enemies, motion included, which matches Tag out today. A status can opt out with pure: true.
    • Untargetable: new shots pass through, area searches and target picks skip you, homing shots in flight fizzle. Statuses already on you keep ticking.
    • Death and respawn clear every status, one line in the engine instead of six resets.
  3. Replication and snapshot. One field per unit, plain JSON, ticks not seconds: 
    statuses: [
	{ key: 'swig.shield',  src: 'A2', from: 1803, until: 1983, amount: 24.5 },
	{ key: 'tangle.slow',  src: 'B1', from: 1790, until: 1910, mag: 0.6 },
	{ key: 'stagger.bank', src: 'A2', from: 1700, until: 1880, data: { payments: [ … ] } },
]
    Behaviour stays in the folder, looked up by key, so a client needs only the list to draw icons, timers, shield slivers and the Stagger pool. Snapshot, both replicas and every reset learn statuses once and never again. Shots carry applies: ['tangle.slow'] instead of slow: { factor, duration }, so a caught shot in Pocket keeps its status by key.
  4. One contact fact, and the folder's own sounds and juice (slice 3). Support contacts (Pass's heal, Tag out, Red light) present one generic contact fact with a part, so index.js, feedback.js, sound-table.js and the bench report learn it once instead of a fact type per ability. Sounds and particles live in the ability's tune file (stats.sound, stats.juice), as Coach's sounds already do. Listeners (on), a zone onLand and beforeHit wait for a folder that needs them: the zone landing is already generic (damage plus the folder's applies), and ability onHit now runs once, before damage.
  5. Bots read the folder (slice 4). An ability's bot is a built-in tactic name, false, or its own { priority, act(ctx, slot) }: 
    bot: 'cone'                               // Tangle (was botCone)
bot: 'radial'                             // Around the world (was botRadial)
bot: false                                // Flying kick: Brew's own bot owns it
bot: { priority: 30, act(ctx, slot) { … } } // Tag out: save a losing ally
    Without bot, the defaults reproduce today's kind-finds (shot, zone, dash, blink, cover, catch), so most folders write nothing. Coach's three tactics move from her hero hook into Pass, Tag out and Red light, so any kit that slots them gets a bot that uses them. The hero bot hook stays for kits like Brew's, whose abilities share one brain. Dropped on the evidence: generated card sentences (no hero ever touched tooltip.js; a folder's own summary costs nothing) and targets metadata (heal and heroOnly already make ally or hero-only shots with no shared file).
  6. Summons (slice 5). sim.summon(owner, key, at): a unit with an owner, a lifetime, statuses, an optional body and a think hook, in one replicated list. Bodiless summons are persistent areas (Chronosphere, Graviton, Void Prison, a lingering Rain). Coach's cutout and Pong's boards port onto it, and outOfPlay becomes "this player controls that summon", which is the one concept targeting.js, hud.js and index.js learn.
  7. What we deliberately won't support. Crowd-control immunity and diminishing returns (Oskar's rule: a stun is a stun). Damage schools and resistances (one damage type). Attributes beyond HP, speed and damage, so no armour or crit chance as stats. Talents (settled in docs/extending.md). Carried units: vehicles, riding, swallowing. Stealth, which needs per-viewer replication on top of fog. Mana or any second resource. Mind control and team changes.

5. Migration, in slices

Every slice ports, never changes behaviour. The proof is the same for each: tests/characterization.test.js snapshots identical, tests/boundaries.test.js green, the moba tests near the change green, and bun run simulate --base <previous> --lineup <touched heroes> --report with every delta exactly zero (same seeds and same behaviour give the same tables, not just overlapping intervals). Rule changes, like strongest-slow-wins, come after as their own commit with their own delta.

  1. Statuses core. Landed (main at rvvxrpzonqpm): statuses.js with absorb, moveSpeed, periodic, the hooks, tags, stacking and cleanse; Brew's shields and Stagger, every slow and Tag out's slow cleanse run on it. Strongest slow wins, as its own commit after the port.
  2. Disables and motion. Landed (main at tuxmwmlynqvn). stunUntil, freezeUntil, proneUntil and displacement become statuses behind one gate table, and Tag out's damage reduction becomes a damageTaken status, which retires the hero-level traits.damageFactor. Port Red light, Tag out, Dive, the Ball's stun, Throw and Around the world. No new states yet: rooted, silenced and untargetable wait for a folder that uses them, so this slice can prove zero deltas. Freeze blocks Ball pickup, like stun. Tag out clears stun, freeze, slow and motion, but leaves Dive's prone cost. Minions stop moving and attacking during the Ball's stun. This is where Hook's ten displacement files go.
  3. Contacts and folder presentation. Landed (main at rplrxmrnoppx). heal, tagOut and freeze facts become one contact; Hook's and Brew's sounds and particles move from look.js into their tune files, Pass's and Red light's feedback into juice. Proof adds a headless dump of the sound board's uses, identical except for the fact rename.
  4. Bots read the folder. Built-in tactics and folder tactics replace the kind-finds, botCone/botRadial, Brew's dash opt-out and Coach's hero hook. Proof: zero deltas, brain-state shares included; the trap is the bots' shared random stream, so code moves but calls never reorder.
  5. Summons. Port Coach's cutout and Pong's boards. Proof adds one replay of a Coach death, played back to the same fingerprint.
