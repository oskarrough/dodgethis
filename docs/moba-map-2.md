# MOBA map 2: Flagfall

The second map, after [moba-lane.md](moba-lane.md) ("the paper lane"). Same heroes, structures, minion stats and heartbeat; two lanes instead of one. The shipped match has waves, structures and lane-aware bots, without the Ball or flags; capture the flag below is planned. Where the numbers differ, this doc wins. Every number goes in the plugin's `tune.js`; map files read those values.

- **Two lanes make the 3v3 choose.** Leaving a lane unsoaked costs 240 of a team's ~960 XP a minute, and a trip to the other lane and back takes at least 12 s, so teams split 2–1 and pay for every rotation.
- **Capture the flag, on brand.** Each team's flag stands on its own side; take theirs home while yours is still there. The Ball pulls everyone to the middle; the flags send the teams past each other diagonally. Bots follow the rule kids use: two go, one stays.

Cover, gaps, pillars, posts, building footprints and dummy posts scale together; hero sizes, movement and ability ranges do not. - **Bounds (squeezed 2026-10-10):** 104 × 32 m (|x| ≤ 52, |z| ≤ 16). Lanes are 6 ≤ |z| ≤ 16; waves and towers walk z ±12.5, the shore side. Between them the yard is |x| ≤ 28, |z| < 4, behind hedges at 4 ≤ |z| ≤ 6 that run 3–8, 12–16 and 20–28 on each side, leaving a 6 m mid gap and four 4 m gaps per lane. A base block fills 28 ≤ |x| ≤ 38, |z| ≤ 6; the lanes enter a full-height courtyard at |x| ≥ 38. The base is |x| ≥ 46 with map 1's healing; heroes spawn at (±49, 0).
- **Dunk (built 2026-10-10):** the shore fence is broken at 5 ≤ |x| ≤ 11 on both shores. The outer 3.5 m of court inside a gap is slick, printed wet with hazard stripes. Loose, Toss or Rain (from its centre) hitting a hero on the slick shoves them 3 m along the hit in 0.2 s, without control; a shove that reaches the edge inside the gap dunks them: they sail out, splash, the ref calls it OUT, the dunker's team gets takedown XP, and they climb out at base 4 s later on the HP they had. No shove while shoved or for 1 s after. Bots: most step off the slick with an enemy hero within 12 m (easy never do), and aim Rain just inland of a hero on it. Numbers in `tune.flagfall.dunk`.
- **Rotation (built 2026-10-10):** on a two-lane map a bot with no enemy hero within 14 m crosses the yard to a teammate's fight within 28 m. The point-symmetric deal (A's pair facing B's single) was tried and dropped: with bots, each pair took a tower at 0:44 and kills halved.
- **Posts and cover:** A's flag at (−16, +3), B's at (+16, −3). Pillars (r 1.1): four in the yard at (±7, ±2), one in front of each tower at (±13, ±13). Nothing stands beside a post.
- **Structures:** per side, a tower in each lane at (±18, ±15), a fort in each at (±32, ±15), and the core at (±42, 0), with map 1's numbers. A fort is vulnerable once its lane's tower falls; the core once both towers and either fort fall.
- **Planned waves:** every 30 s each lane gets half a wave, spawned at (±40, ±15) and walking z ±15 in two files: 2 melee and 1 ranged in one lane, 1 melee, 1 ranged and the wizard in the other, swapping each wave. From 10:00 the brute alternates lanes.

## Capture the flag

- **Rounds:** hoists land on a fixed clock whatever the last round did: the first round opens at 3:00, then every 2:30 (1:30 from 10:00). For 30 s before, both flags climb their poles. A round lasts 60 s; then flags go home and the poles lower. A capture ends the round early, and the poles stay down until the next hoist.
- **Steal:** a 1 s channel within 1 m of the enemy post, interrupted by damage. Any owner within 3 m of their own post contests it: no channel starts. The carrier moves at ×0.85 and can't attack, cast or mount. Death, a stun or any teleport drops the flag, and Tag out never picks a carrier. A dropped flag goes home after 8 s or when an owner touches it; the enemy can re-steal it after 1 s.
- **Capture:** the carrier reaches its own post while its own flag is home; otherwise it waits and `flagDenied` says why. Payoff: 300 team XP, and the losing team's frontmost vulnerable structure in each lane is silenced: 20 s for a tower, 30 s for a fort, 35 s for the core (counted once).

| ---------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0:00–3:00  | Early  | Both lanes; first half waves 0:15                                               |
| 3:00–10:00 | Rounds | Hoist 30 s, round up to 60 s, lull to the next hoist; the HUD counts to either  |
| 10:00–     | Late   | Waves every 20 s, brute alternating lanes; a hoist every 1:30 on the same clock |

## Why these numbers

- **Lanes 30 m apart:** a one-way rotation through a post gap is at least 6 s at 5 m/s, and the camera sees about 21 m of depth, so nobody sees both lanes. The yard centre is 15 m from each lane, outside the 12 m soak.
- **Posts at (∓16, ±3):** every point of the 3 m contest circle is at least 9.2 m from a tower's centre, past its 8.2 m reach to a hero, and the nearest cover, a hedge corner, is 2.4 m outside the circle. A guard contests in the open or not at all, and stealing is never a dive. A guard pushed off the post still interrupts the 1 s channel with a basic a second, so a steal takes two. Walk 4 s, channel 1 s, carry 8 s: three or four attempts fit a 60 s round.
- **The carry:** posts are 32.6 m apart in a straight line, which clips the yard pillars at (±7, ±2), so a carry is about 8 s. One chaser gains 0.75 m/s walking and 3.1 m per Vault, so an unescorted carrier is caught; escorts and cooldowns decide it, not the head start.
- **Silence by tier:** walking from your post to an enemy tower takes 7–8 s. Two level-4 Fletchers with a half wave need about 9.8, 20.3 and 24.4 s for a tower, fort and core, so each silence is travel plus that plus 2 s. A solo needs 16.5 s for a tower, so the other lane is a feint, not a threat: the silence makes the defenders choose a lane, and three defenders should hold it.

## Bots, online and look

- **Two go, one stays.** The flag step replaces the Ball step: dodge, retreat, flag, fight, push, lane. Roles lock at the hoist and change only on death. Between rounds: one ally per lane, the second in the lane toward the enemy post. **Guard:** holds within 3 m of our post while our flag is home. **Thieves:** go for the enemy post; if a guard contests, they fight the guard with the advantage check waived (2v1 is the plan; tower safety still applies), then channel. **Recovery:** our flag taken, the guard and any thief not carrying chase the carrier. **Carrier:** walks home by the Ball carrier's path rule and, if our flag is away, holds 5 m behind our post; nothing but dodge overrides it. After our capture, all three go to the silenced structure the pair can finish; after theirs, all three defend the nearer one.
- **Online** waits for M6: moba's `apply` still returns false. The start message carries the map id beside the tune hash. Judge whole envelopes, not the flags delta: four more structure records already cost about 0.7 KB idle, and two independent lanes change wave and fact peaks.
- **Look:** the same game in a different place. Flagfall reuses Overthrow's pale tarmac, chalk, torn rock edge and cover meshes through `match-terrain.js` and `map.js`, with shared broken chain-link from `lobby-floor.js`. Both lanes, the yard and the base courtyards sit on one playing surface; the scaled layout sits in clear teal-violet shallows instead of dunes. A generated background plate (`public/scenery/flagfall-water.webp`, 2400 × 1000, 208 KB) covers a water plane sized uniformly from `tune.flagfall.water.width`; its native 2.4:1 aspect is never stretched. Flat teal-violet water remains if the image is unavailable. Fixed pools add slow broken foam parallel to the long edges, a gentle surface shimmer, rock-edge rings, bobbing lily pads and soft cloud wisps outside the court. These are presentation only and freeze with pause; clouds are the first effect to drop under frame pressure. All geometry controls apply on restart. No new palette roles, camera, units or HUD. Oskar's 2026-10-09 shallow-water references guide the surround, not the playing surface. Posts are plain poles in the lane match; the planned team pennant climbs during the hoist, flies above a carrier and gets an edge pip.

## The seam: maps are data plus one objective

Maps live in `src/plugins/moba/maps/{overthrow,flagfall,lobby}.js`. `mapLayout()` builds one run-owned description from tune: `name`, `bounds`, `obstacles`, `boxes`, `pillars`, team `spawns`, `spawnSpacing`, `bases`, `lanes: [{ id, path: [{ x, z }, …], halfWidth? }]`, `structures: [{ id, team, kind, x, z, lane?, after? }]` and `dummyPosts`. Flagfall also carries its scaled `settings` and flag `posts`. Optional `walkingBounds` keeps the lobby and Flagfall's soft walking clamp separate from terrain walls; the lobby's `fieldBounds` retains its open shooting field. The sim reads bounds, obstacles and spawns from the layout; sim colliders and lane structure bodies share its structure spots. Geometry helper exports remain Overthrow defaults for existing callers.

`src/plugins/moba/maps/index.js` is the map registry: each entry supplies `layout`, object `pieces`, `palette`, `debugTune` and `online`. Overthrow is map + structures + minions + Ball + bots; Flagfall is map + structures + minions + bots; the lobby is map + dummies. Piece objects in `match.js` carry their constructors, views, bot habits and optional rules; structures and minions share one lane population. Callers pass `...matchRecipe(map)` to `createSim`, including headless matches; there is no legacy `lane:` preset. The Ball receives a target hook rather than a lane stub, and publishes its own snapshot clock even without structures. The minimap draws either chalk footprints or live buildings according to the structures piece. Each enabled wave spawns a full population per lane, files along its normal, and follows its path in team order before turning toward the enemy core. Lane path endpoints are the wave spawns, separate from hero spawns. Structure positions and prerequisite `after` chains come from the layout; kind stats come from tune. An `after` list requires every named structure to fall; `{ all, any }` combines all-of and any-of gates. Base healing and passive XP use the layout's base positions, and minion planning uses its bounds. Ground prints retain their tuning reads. The bot split is built: `bots.js` keeps the core recipe, with hero combat/evasion helpers and lane/Ball habits beside their match pieces. Flagfall's core requires both towers and either fort. Splitting a wave between lanes remains planned. Bots receive lane ids and paths through the delayed view. A separate seeded deal spreads allied bots across lanes, without reserving a lane for an idle human. Advance, siege and defence select their own lane's population by path progress; a cleared lane joins any remaining core prerequisite. The shared core is approached through the enemy courtyard. `maps/paths.js` owns projection and goals, including the original lane's exact horizontal arithmetic. Bots accept an active module list as `createBots(seats, seed, modules)` (`[]` by default). The neutral start/reset state is `advance`, independent of modules; Overthrow's old `lane` state maps one-to-one to `advance`. A module provides `botView(sim, unit)` and a per-brain `createBot()` with optional `botPerceive(ctx)`, `botThreats(ctx)` and named `botGoals`. Views enter the same delayed ring as heroes. Perception returns contributions: the core concatenates blockers, objectives and target preferences, sums pressure, combines safety predicates with every, ORs own skill/cast constraints and multiplies movement scales. Private habit memory never enters the shared context. Goals write ordinary intents and return the frame to claim a phase, or null to defer; duplicate phase owners are rejected. The goal plan preserves Overthrow's existing ordering: returnShot, retreat, urgent, selectTarget, commitSiege, objective, defend, fight, escort, siege, hold, advance, push. A paid wave screen commits before the Ball; an unclaimed siege follows combat. Ball habits read public objective contributions, publish their own nextBall view and retain Overthrow's flank tuning (reachable only through the Ball piece, which Flagfall omits); a carrier without a target defers instead of freezing. The sim selects only the active lane/Ball habits on both bot creation and reset.

Headless proof with the practice roster on Normal, six bot-controlled seats and seeds 1–3: core kills at 12:45.97, 11:52.85 and 10:58.40. Both lanes had minion-screen sieges and bot structure hits in every match. A separate seed-2 match left the human seat on neutral input: allies pushed both lanes and the core fell at 6:13.82. The longest bot move-order stall was 1.02 s (less than 0.1 m travelled while a distant move goal remained pending, excluding death, casts and attacks). Sampled median sim steps were 0.18–0.42 ms, well inside one frame; these are headless logic timings, not rendered FPS or proof of human winnability. The four-match Overthrow comparison, including its ability and bot-state report, had zero deltas. No browser proof; Oskar plays the linked route above.

The planned objective interface is `begin`, `control`, `carry(h) → { speed, range }`, `hurt`, `intercept(context) → consumed`, `finish`, `snapshot`, `heroState(h)` and `botGoal`; until that extraction, `sim.js` still names the Ball.

## Build order

1. **The map-data seam:** `bun run simulate --summary --base --cross-only --matches 4 --jobs 2` gives zero deltas on the same seeds; dodgeball characterization and existing MOBA sim/lane tests stay green. A headless boot keeps Flagfall's map working. No new unit tests, component split or bot-brain changes in this step.
2. **Flagfall lane match (built)** (`?mode=moba&play&map=flagfall&hero=fletcher&bots=normal`): the scaled court, cover, shallow water and team spawns, plus towers and forts in both lanes and a shared core per team. Each lane gets a full wave. Normal bots split across both lanes, escort waves, siege and defend by lane progress; allies still push with the human standing still. The match ends on a core kill. No Ball, flags or yard dummies. Online Flagfall stays refused. Map selection survives restart and the crane; Overthrow retains its seeded outcomes.
3. **Flags:** the fixed hoist clock survives an early capture; contest, interrupt, carry speed and no casts; death, stun and Tag out drop or skip the flag; the 8 s return and owner touch; a capture refused while our flag is away, then accepted; silence per tier, one per lane, core once. Matchups: a Vaulting Fletcher catches an unescorted 6 m lead; Mitts' W never stops a basic or Rain aimed at a carrier. An undefended pair finishes each tier inside its silence, three defenders stop it, and a solo can't finish a tower. `scripts/verify-moba-flags.mjs` screenshots hoist, steal, carry, capture and silence at 390, 1440 and 2560 × 1080.
4. **Bots (after 04):** six normal bots, seeds 1–3: each seed captures at least twice before 10:00 and ends in a core kill within 15 minutes; with both guards up, thieves break a guard in at least half the rounds; a carrier never gets conflicting goals when both flags are out. If a seed runs long, shorten the round interval first.
5. **Online (after M6):** 3v3 envelope p95 at most 7 KB, measured with both lanes unsoaked and with two simultaneous sieges.
