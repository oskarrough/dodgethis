# MOBA front end

The splash leads straight into the [playable plaza](moba-lobby.md), then loading, the match and post-match. The plaza's cardboard hero stands and difficulty gallery replace the difficulty screen, hero select, kit card and `front/preview.js`. Back from the plaza returns to the splash. The in-game look is [moba-look.md](moba-look.md). `plugins/moba/front/` owns the splash, backdrop, loading and numbers sheet; `lobby.js` owns the playable entrance.

**The two looks share an ink and differ in weight.** The front end is the world, drawn in ligne claire: one thin, even navy line, pale fills, fine hatching where the shade falls, and a lot of empty sky. The match is a toy printed from that world: thick ink, loud flat colour, halftone, and a crowded ground. Both use the same navy `ink`, the same `cream` and the same team colours. The front-end pastels come from the palette roles mixed 60–85% toward cream, with the far planes palest, so they add no new roles. The loading descent is where one look becomes the other: the camera falls from the horizon to the lane while the line thickens and the colours saturate. Keeping them this far apart is deliberate. The quiet in the front end is what makes the match feel loud.

**Feel comes before the line.** The menus answer like a console game (Smash: snappy, chunky, instant) and are made like a toy (Toy Story, Sackboy: tactile, warm, handmade). They meet Moebius by being cut out of his world: each tile is a cardboard sticker pinned on the desert, drawn in the same navy ink and cream, with a stitched seam and a hard printed shadow. The landscape stays ligne claire and quiet; only the things you can touch are thick, saturated and springy. That is the same weight split as the front end and the match, one level down.

## Core changes (approved, opt-in, scoped)

- **Style preset:** `setStylePreset({ line, hatch, alpha })` returns a restore function, and the run scope calls it on dispose. `alpha` clears the sky and blits transparent. `hatch` is new shader logic: world-anchored lines replace the halftone dots, and the cream bands are dropped. The defaults are today's pass, byte for byte. Tests: the characterization snapshots stay identical, and a pixel diff of a fixed dodgeball frame, before and after a preset-and-restore round trip, shows nothing.
- **Render gate:** `app.renderDemand(fn)`, scoped the same way. `browser.js` skips `view.render()` while any demand says no, but it keeps running `app.frame`, so menu input is still polled. Verify zero draw calls on model-free screens with `renderer.info`.
- **Tune scope:** front/backdrop controls live above the runs; gameplay controls belong to each run. The open numbers sheet reads live tune and the current hero's definition, and refreshes on an edit.
- **Lettering:** Josefin Sans (OFL), self-hosted as two woff2 subsets (light and regular, Latin only) in `public/fonts/`, and preloaded.

## The shared backdrop

- One backdrop is handed from splash to plaza to loading, and loading cancellation reuses it: a vast pastel desert under a sky that fills three quarters of the frame. The sky is two or three near-flat CSS colour bands from peach through mint to pale lilac, with a huge pale planet low on the left and long, inked strata of cloud.
- The dunes, mesas and one broken arch are hand-written inline SVG paths. Hatching follows the form: pixel-sized 0.75 px pattern lines run vertically on mesa shade faces, and contour strokes follow the dunes. Every plane uses `xMidYMax slice`, with one uniform hatch counter-scale. Fletcher stands on the ridge against the planet, with a long printed shadow.
- Compositing budget: four parallax layers, none rastered larger than the viewport plus 10%. Hatching never scales. The descent scales only a pre-rasterized, unhatched copy at device-pixel resolution, and crossfades to it first. Each screen gets its own sky by crossfading two stacked gradient layers. At most six animated surfaces are alive at once, and that count includes View Transition snapshots, so a transition only runs while the backdrop is still. There's no `filter`, no `backdrop-filter`, and no animated gradient or shadow.
- Type: Darumadrop (`--ui-font`) sets only the big words (VICTORY, mode names, READY). Josefin Sans sets everything else, with tabular figures for numbers. Sound: one airy synth chord per confirm, and a low wind bed from `music.js`.

## Screens

- **Splash:** a fresh `/` starts with two sticker tiles, Dodgeball and MOBA, without default link parameters. MOBA and the hub's MOBA portal open the plaza; `?mode=moba` does too. Incoming `hero=` and `bots=` select the plaza's initial picks. `?mode=moba&play&hero=mitts&bots=hard` bypasses it and starts the match as Mitts on Hard. Hover or focus lifts, untilts and floods a tile inside a frame. Esc or B on the splash has nowhere to go and shakes the tiles. Prompts follow the last device; mouse gets no key badges.
- **Inspection and numbers:** the plaza uses the match's HUD tooltips for dummies and presentation-only targets for hero stands, difficulty cards and ready-bot standees. Hover gives a nameplate; hold Alt for its card, or aim and hold Y on pad. N, View or the `#` button opens `front/numbers.js` for the current hero. It holds the sim and presentation, including dummy strafing, tells and recovery. Scroll with stick, arrows, wheel or touch; prompts follow the active device. No inspect card opens merely because the pointer was already over the new HUD. B, Esc, N or View closes it without leaving the plaza. Closing consumes the gesture and requires fresh gameplay input. The sheet uses the same live card builders as the HUD and keeps the acceleration-aware flight/dodge estimates in `front/stats.js`. Planned Volley is labelled not playable. The chalk boxes and Ready input belong to [moba-lobby.md](moba-lobby.md).
- **Loading:** the two teams stand as rows of small figures on facing ridges, with the map's name in the sky. The map builds behind the screen with the sim frozen. The descent starts only when the map is ready, a 1.8 s hold has run out, and the ready establishing shot has had at least 0.8 s of dwell. A confirm press during the hold skips the remaining hold and dwell but never the readiness wait. That press is consumed, so it can't reach the match. The **descent** takes 0.8 s: the backdrop scales and fades while the camera follows a real arc, rising to about 50 m before falling onto the local participant's follow camera. Pitch settles before the final approach, and the preset tweens to the sticker values. The whole floor fits the establishing shot at every aspect ratio; the figures use the backdrop's ridge projection. The loading UI fades away in the first 28% of the descent, and Skip is disabled as soon as the hold ends. The sim, the clock and match input stay frozen until it lands. Key releases still reach core input, and the actual follow spring is primed before control resumes. Back during loading cancels and returns to the plaza, preserving the map, backdrop and current picks.
- **Match cards (built):** Esc or controller Start opens the shared sticker overlay with Resume, Restart, Hero select and Modes. Esc, B or Start resumes. Resume is focused first; pointer hover cannot change the choice until the pointer moves, and Space never confirms a card. Pause is disabled until loading lands. Core destruction freezes the simulation immediately, then the camera focuses the fallen core while its shatter and Ball burst finish. The result freezes presentation and shows VICTORY or DEFEAT relative to the local participant, with Again, Hero and Modes. Again and Restart start a fresh match directly; Hero returns to the plaza, Modes to the splash. The full post-match below is still planned.
- **Post-match (planned):** it opens after the core shatter. VICTORY or DEFEAT fills a dusk sky in the team colour. Below it is a ligne-claire table with one row per hero: takedowns, deaths, hero damage, siege damage, Ball hits and XP soaked. A team XP graph in SVG marks structure kills and Balls. Buttons: _Again_ (same seats, straight to loading), _Hero_ and _Modes_. Counters come from sim facts, never from scraping the DOM.

## Build order

One thread per slice. Each formats and lints its own files, runs nearby tests (the orchestrator runs `bun run check` at checkpoints), completes its flow with keyboard only, pad only and mouse only, and backs out one screen per Esc or B. Screenshots are at 1440 × 900, 1280 × 720 and 2560 × 1080.

1. **Core, backdrop and mode select:** the preset and render-gate APIs, the fonts, the desert, and Practice starting directly with a plain cut. Screenshots: each card focused by pad. Traces: 10 s idle with no layout or paint per frame, zero draw calls, and a sky crossfade at 2560 × 1440 and DPR 2.
2. **Playable entrance (built):** [the plaza](moba-lobby.md) supplies hero stands, a difficulty gallery, chalk Ready boxes, inspection and the suspended numbers sheet. The hero-select screen, kit card and portrait preview are removed.
3. **Loading and descent:** gates, frozen sim, skip, cancel. Tests: held confirm, early skip, a slow build (stubbed at 3 s), cancel mid-load. Screenshots at 0%, 50% and 100% of the descent. Trace at 2560 × 1440 and DPR 2, checking raster work and hatch shimmer.
4. **Versus AI:** six claimed chalk seats and a full 3v3 through loading.
5. **Post-match**, after the core shatter (moba-lane.md build step 6, which ships first): the dusk sky, table, graph and buttons. Screenshots: victory and defeat from a seeded bot match, with pad focus on _Again_. Test: _Again_ starts clean, with the pools empty and the clock at 0:00.
