# PvE

Altamesa is played alone or with up to four others, bots or friends in any mix, and the Wardens must be a fair fight for whoever turns up; a bot counts as a player.

Two questions sit under everything else. Does the dungeon meet you, so the fight fits the party, its skill and its mood? Does it remember you, so the next visit is not the same as the last? Difficulty tiers and scaling by party size are answers to the first; boons in a run and talents that last are answers to the second. Neither list is settled.

What remembers you should reward curiosity, never attendance: no streaks, timers or chests, and nothing a child feels they must log in for. New choices are better than bigger numbers.

Candidates still open: a tier ladder as in Delves, boons picked after each Warden, talents unlocked by fallen Wardens, mastery itself as the only progress, and the mesa changing where a Warden fell.
