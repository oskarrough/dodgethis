# MOBA lobby

A MOBA map tile from the splash opens a place you play in, like the LittleBigPlanet pod: you run around the lobby as your hero, swap heroes from the strip at the left, try the kit on dummies, shoot a difficulty card and walk into your Ready box. The 3D lobby fills the frame above the HUD; the hero strip stays at the left, apart from stickers on the props they name. The map is already picked on the splash; the lobby contains only heroes, difficulty and Ready. Esc goes back to the [splash](moba-front.md).

**Oskar's rules (2026-10-10).** The lobby is its own world: the same lobby whichever map you picked, but it shows which map you're about to enter. Bots stand on their seats as holograms; keep them. Difficulty stays magnets you shoot, restyled into our world. Picked direction: Pocket weather ([concept](pages/lobby/04-pocket-weather-v2.webp)): a huge ceramic saucer in magnetic cradles; a small turning diorama in a ceramic weather bowl previews the next map, and only the bowl's miniature, sky cap and label change. No stock fantasy: generic D&D-Steam props (training dummies, banner plinths, rune stones) read as AI filler.

**Moebius, with weight.** Moebius sets the line, the light and the empty sky. The isles set what things are made of: stone, bark and bone, weathered, per [moba-look.md](moba-look.md); today's sack dummies are the last of the retired schoolyard look. The line is drawn; the props are built. They squash when bumped, rock when hit and settle with a little overshoot.

## The space and the sim (built)

- **The lobby is its own small map, the same whichever map comes next:** `createMapScope().start(run, create, 'lobby')` builds a huge glazed saucer floating in the splash sky (the splash plate under its dusk wash). Its walkable glaze is an ellipse holding the walking bounds with over 1 m to spare at their corners: cream glaze, faint crazing, iron speckle, a few chips to the biscuit, a darker ring where the glaze pools at the well. A rounded lip carries a painted restaurant-ware band; under it, a thick unglazed violet body tucks in to a foot ring. Three iron horseshoe magnets with cream poles hold the rim in their jaws with a hand's width of air, thin ink on every silhouette (`lobby-floor.js`, `tune.lobby.floor`, applies on restart). Its collider is the same ellipse out to the lip (`buildColliders`, `island.round`); walk past the lip and you fall, then drop back in. The camera is fixed at the lane's 58° pitch and fits all walkable ground above the HUD; portrait widens the lens instead of shrinking the hero.
- **The weather bowl shows the next map:** a glazed bowl on the saucer's far right holds the map picked on the splash in miniature, built from that map's own layout (court, lanes, walls, hedges, pillars, towers and cores with team tips), turning slowly over a cloud bed with a painted sky arch behind: Overthrow by day (butter-green court, peach sky, pale moon, far isles), Flagfall by night (violet court, teal hedges, crescent, stars). Only the miniature, the sky and the label change; the bowl is the same. The label prints flush-left on the saucer under it, a small-caps caption over the map name, no frame. Reduced motion holds it still (`lobby-bowl.js`, `tune.lobby.bowl`).
- **The map outlives the lobby:** map and Rapier world live in a MOBA-level scope. The crane rebuilds the terrain as the lane on the same world; restart reuses it; leaving MOBA disposes it. One sim is alive at a time, through `map.start(run, world => createSim(...))`.
- **The lobby sim is `createSim({ ...matchRecipe('lobby'), lobby: true, readyRoster, spawns, respawn })`:** one lobby mark per seat, walking clamped to `tune.lobby.bounds`, 0.5 s recovery on your mark. Real heroes, two sparring dummies, the match's views and HUD; no towers, minions or Ball. Humans fight as team A and dummies as B, so players can't hit each other; each hero keeps its picked team as `seatTeam`.
- **The roster carries picks:** `practiceRoster(local, difficulty, picks)` takes `{ seatId: { heroId, team } }`. Picks survive the crane, restart and the `hero=` link. `parseMatchSetup` accepts any hero `heroes.js` marks `playable`.

## Picking and trying

- **Heroes sit in a strip down the left edge,** apart from the HUD. Modestly enlarged, thick-bordered tiles scale to the viewport in portrait and desktop layouts. One tile per `heroes.js` entry; yours is the big one, framed gold. Click another tile, press H or d-pad up to swap; locked tiles say "soon", wobble and deny. Click your own tile, press N or View to open its numbers sheet. The keyboard badge (H or ✛↑) sits on the strip and nowhere else.
- **Maps are picked on the splash:** the chosen `setup.map` and `?map=` survive leave → lobby, Again and reload. There are no map tiles, M shortcut or d-pad left/right map actions in the lobby. Ready fills unchanged; the descent letters the chosen map name and lands on it.
- **The toy moment is the flip:** the body turns edge-on like a cutout spun on a pin, then comes round as the new hero in 0.25 s, mid-stride, and the name slams on.
- **`swapHero(id, heroId)` keeps the hero object and id** and swaps body and costume in place, emitting a `swap` fact. It cancels everything armed (queued presses, orders, casts, channels, catches, dashes, held aim), resets cooldowns, keeps level and HP fraction, and carries walk velocity clamped to the new speed. Shots already out finish. Locked, unknown or dead picks deny. Lobby only.
- **Difficulty is a shooting gallery of magnets:** Easy, Normal and Hard are floating horseshoe magnets at the near edge, out of the dummy lanes, so kids who can't read can still tell them apart. Easy is green and sleepy with one ball on a slow lazy loop; Normal is orange and calm with two balls on a brisk orbit; Hard is red, scowling and crackling, with three balls buzzing round tight and fast. Any cast or basic whose footprint touches a magnet within 2 m of its aim picks it, once per cast; a nearer live dummy vetoes, and ties go by footprint centre, then id. The picked magnet's balls slam onto its poles with a clack, stick for a beat, then spin back out into orbit, over an ink ring and an emphasized label; the others droop, lose their pull and drop their balls to roll on the floor until picked again, when the balls leap back up. Bot holograms in empty seats wear the picked magnet's face. G or d-pad down is the autopilot: your hero walks to the firing mark and fires a harmless chalk shot at the next magnet. Moving or Esc cancels it. The picked magnet carries the only G badge (`lobby-magnets.js`, `tune.lobby.gallery.magnet`).
- **Inspection:** hover for a nameplate, hold Alt or Y for the card, as in the match. The numbers sheet (`front/numbers.js`) suspends sim and presentation, scrolls with stick, arrows or wheel, and closes on B, Esc, N or View without leaving. No prompt advertises Alt.

## Input and Ready

- **One meaning per key:** RMB, A, QWER and the digits keep their kit meaning. Enter or Start means Ready. Esc or B peels one layer: the open sheet, then the Ready walk or fill, then the autopilot shot, then a held or active cast, then back to the splash. Presses held across a screen change are ignored until released. The HUD strip lists only move and the kit. The player frame (portrait, HP and XP) sits top-left, at the same position and size as in the match; Back sits directly beneath it, above the hero strip.
- **Ready is part of the saucer (built):** six plates inlaid in the glaze, three per team: an ink groove, a band of team glaze, a cream well. Bots float over theirs as team holograms. Enter, Start or clicking your plate walks you there; stand on it for 1 s while gold glaze runs out from the middle of the well, and the crane starts. Step out or press Esc and it empties. Until you stand on it, your plate has Ready and its key (Enter, or Start on a pad) printed beside it on the saucer's outer side (`lobby-props.js`, `tune.lobby.ready`). The sim owns `readySeats`: claims go through `readySeats.claim(seatId, occupant, tick)`, an earlier tick wins, then join order, then id; a human displaces a bot to a free plate.
- **Online (future):** until MOBA online lands, the lobby refuses shared sessions and returns to the splash with a notice. Later the lobby is what an online room looks like: the host runs the lobby sim, and walking to the other team's line claims a seat there.
