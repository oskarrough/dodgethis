# MOBA lobby

MOBA from the splash opens a place you play in, like the LittleBigPlanet pod: run around the plaza, pick a cardboard hero stand, try the kit on dummies, shoot the difficulty gallery and walk to the start line when ready. The plaza replaces difficulty, hero select, the kit card and `front/preview.js` from [moba-front.md](moba-front.md). Back returns to the splash. Incoming hero/difficulty picks still work, and `play` keeps its direct-match bypass.

**Moebius and Sackboy.** Moebius sets the line, the light and the empty sky: thin navy ink, pastels mixed toward cream, hatching in the shade. Pixar, Toy Story and Sackboy set what things are made of. Everything you can touch is a handmade object with weight: cardboard stands, stuffed sack dummies, a chalk start line, tape on the floor. The line is drawn; the props are built. Props squash when bumped, rock when hit and settle with a little overshoot. The match then prints the same toys loud.

## The space and the sim

- **The plaza of the real lane**, between the hedges (±12 m), on the front-end preset (`hatch: 1`, thin line, pastels, alpha over the SVG desert). The road runs out of frame both ways and the camera never follows it. The playable inner court is now compact, with closer props and the lane's 58° pitch. The camera stays map-clamped and fits the real HUD's reserved height, including short landscape viewports, to show every walkable spot above it, with the planet and sky above the far edge. Portrait widens the lens only enough to fit the compact court rather than shrinking the hero to fit the old plaza.
- **The lobby sim is `createSim({ lane: false, lobby: true, readyRoster, spawns, bounds, respawn })`**: `spawns` is `{ participantId: { x, z } }`, one plaza mark per seat; `bounds` is `{ halfX, halfZ }`, clamping walking, orders and dashes one body radius inside the plaza; `respawn` is hero recovery in seconds (0.5 s on your mark, not 8 s). `tune.lobby` supplies bounds (±4 m across, ±6.2 m deep), recovery and six printed marks for the caller to assign. The marks at x ±1.4 m and z −1.25/0/+1.25 m clear the pillars and remain visible above the HUD at 390 and 1440 wide. Omitted options keep match and training behaviour unchanged; dummies keep their own recovery timer. The real heroes, the two sparring dummies and the match's views and HUD; no towers, minions or Ball. Headless, `buildMap` takes about 20 ms and the sim about 10 ms; browser shader warm-up is measured in slice 4.
- **The map outlives the lobby.** The map and Rapier world now live in a MOBA-level scope, built on the first plaza or direct-play entry. Loading cancellation also reuses the plaza's backdrop, not just its map. Ready disposes the lobby sim, and loading builds the lane sim on the same world. Cancel returns to the lobby with the map intact, and restart reuses it. Leaving MOBA disposes it. One sim is alive at a time: future lobby runs must also use `map.start(run, world => createSim(...))`, which rejects overlap and disposes the sim on run abort.
- **The roster carries picks.** `practiceRoster(local, difficulty, picks)` now takes `{ seatId: { heroId, team } }`; unpicked seats default to Fletcher. Picks stay in match setup across loading, cancel and restart. `parseMatchSetup` accepts any hero `heroes.js` marks `playable`, and loading, cancel, restart and the `hero=` deep link all pass it through.

## Picking and trying

- **Heroes are cardboard stands** near the gallery, one per `heroes.js` entry. Half-size cutouts lean back 45°, keeping your body visible as you approach. Walk onto a playable stand and your body swaps there. A locked stand shows its silhouette and a "soon" tag, and nudges you off with the deny wobble.
- **The toy moment is the flip.** Run onto Mitts's stand and the body turns edge-on like a cutout spun on a pin, then comes round as Mitts in 0.25 s, mid-stride. Your shadow changes shape a beat first.
- **`swapHero(id, heroId)` keeps the hero object and id**, so views, HUD and bots holding it stay valid. It replaces the body with the new profile and costume at the same position and facing, and emits a `swap` fact (`hero`, `from`, `heroId`, `point`, `tick`); the hero retains that fact as `swapFact` for frame-driven HUD and skill views. Swapping is lobby-only; match and training sims deny it. Movement orders replan for the new body, and held RMB and pad movement keep working without resetting the device. It drops queued presses and cancels attack orders, windups, casts, channels, catch windows and dashes, and clears Pocket, Bag, held aim and timed disables. An aim held across a swap cannot cast the new kit on release; release then press afresh, even after repeated swaps. Shots and zones already out finish as they are, and cooldowns reset, including a basic ready on the next tick. Motion transfers explicitly: walk velocity is read before stopping, clamped to the new speed, and written through `body.setVelocity({ x, y, z })`; dash momentum stops rather than transferring. Level and HP fraction stay. Locked, unknown or dead picks deny without replacing anything.
- **Trying the kit:** the real kit at real numbers, on the dummies. One casts harmless Loose back every 4 s behind an inked 0.8 s tell above the plaza print, so dodging is practice too. With `lobby: true`, all humans use combat `team: 'A'` and dummies `'B'`, so players can't hit each other. Each hero and its snapshot keep the picked team as separate `seatTeam` state.
- **Difficulty is a shooting gallery:** Easy, Normal and Hard standees at the near edge, behind the hero stands and away from the dummy practice lanes. The incoming choice is already upright on arrival; other cards start flat. Standees are lobby props, not combatants. A cast picks one when its footprint touches it and its aim point was within 2 m of it: shot contact, a Rain zone, a Vault path or landing, a basic. A live dummy nearer the original aim point than the card vetoes a pick. RMB becomes a basic only within card radius plus `tune.orders.pick`; casts keep their 2 m rule. That makes one `pick` per cast, and on a tie, nearest the footprint centre wins. The chosen standee stays up and the others clatter flat. The sim's optional `footprint` observer carries the original aim and one token through a cast's shots, zone, dash path/landing or catch window; a successful contact closes that token, so overlapping footprints and a continuing flight cannot pick twice. Exact distance ties use the difficulty id, never enumeration order. Basics aim at props without registering them as enemies. G or d-pad down walks to the clear firing mark and fires a harmless chalk shot at the next standee; The chalk shot stops at the standee; any final projectile fact (pick, hit, blocked or expired) clears the shortcut. Esc/B cancels a pending shot without leaving. Start/Enter waits for that shot's contact or final fact before auto-walking to the Ready box with the current difficulty.

## Input context and Ready

- **The lobby owns its bindings.** A, LB/RB, QWER and the digits keep their kit meaning. Start is Ready, since there's nothing to pause. B or Esc cancels Ready navigation or an in-progress Ready fill (and held kit input) first; otherwise it cancels held aim, an active cast, channel or stance, and goes back to the splash only when none is active. Shortcuts that act as if you walked there: H or d-pad up cycles heroes (with the flip), G or d-pad down cycles difficulty (a shot flies to the standee). Hold Alt or hold Y inspects, as in the match. N, View or the `#` button opens the current hero's numbers sheet, which suspends the lobby sim and presentation, scrolls with stick, arrows or wheel, and closes on B, Esc, N or View (that B is consumed). Hover and inspect include hero stands, difficulty cards, ready-bot standees and the real dummies; the props are presentation targets, never combatants. Pad inspection has a plaza-sized aim reach rather than the basic attack's range. A press held across a screen change is ignored until it's released. Walking needs a mouse or pad, as in the match. Every choice also has a key. H/G and d-pad hints are visible on the HUD, H swaps show the picked name and play the pick sound, and touch shows no keyboard-only prompts. Catch cancellation is plaza-only; match cancellation semantics are unchanged.
- **Ready is the start line (built):** chalk across the road on both base sides, with six seat boxes, three per team. Stand in your claimed box for 1 s and its gold print fills smoothly; step out and it empties. Enter, Start, clicking Ready, or A near the Ready prompt walks your hero to its box. A elsewhere keeps its basic-attack meaning. Moving or cancelling interrupts auto-walk; cancelling inside a box clears the fill and requires a fresh Ready or stepping out before it can fill again. The five practice bots stand in filled boxes as uniformly scaled cardboard cutouts of their picked heroes, not combatants. Loading starts only when every active human has a claimed, full box, carrying the current hero, gallery difficulty and claimed teams. The sim owns `readySeats` and `snapshot().readySeats`, with `readyWalk` on the human snapshot. Ready and cancel are intents; after physics, `step()` claims boxes occupied by humans who don't own them, carrying the claimed team into setup. A displaced bot moves to the human's previous or another vacant box. Claims go through `readySeats.claim(seatId, { id, heroId, bot, joinOrder }, tick)`; an earlier tick wins, then join order, then participant id. Humans can replace bot claims; moving a claim clears the old box. This is the same six-seat state the [shared plaza](moba-plan.md#shared-play-m6) can claim later. The only overlay UI is the HUD, tooltips, the Ready and Back glyphs, and the numbers sheet.
- **Online (future):** until MOBA online lands, the plaza refuses shared sessions (host or guest) and returns to the front with a local-practice notice. Players will find each other through the existing online plugin's rooms (`src/plugins/online`: public list, join by code, host authority); the plaza is what a room looks like once you're in. The host runs the lobby sim and replicates it like a match, with no lobby code knowing about the network. Other players walk about with name stickers. Walking to the other team's line claims a free seat on that team. The host owns seat claims and readiness, and the earlier claim tick wins, with join order breaking a tie.

## Build slices: contracts with headless proofs first, feel and browser proof last

No new unit tests (Oskar): each "Tests:" below is a throwaway headless script or browser trace whose output goes in the report.

1. **Map scope (built):** map and world above the run. Tests: loading reuses them, cancel and restart keep them, leaving MOBA disposes them, and two sims never overlap.
2. **Roster (built):** `picks` through `practiceRoster`, `playable` in `parseMatchSetup`, then loading, cancel, restart and `hero=`. Tests: a picked hero survives each path.
3. **Lobby sim options (built):** `spawns`, `bounds`, `respawn`, allegiance and seat team. Tests: spawn on marks, can't leave the plaza, 0.5 s recovery, no friendly hits.
4. **Lobby run (built):** the splash's MOBA tile opens the plaza as the selected hero with the two sparring dummies, the front preset and fixed 58° plaza framing (FOV widened on portrait screens to fit the playable bounds). Its input context ignores inherited presses until release; B/Esc cancels held aim, a cast, channel or stance first, otherwise returns to the splash. Start/Enter now auto-walks to the Ready box and its 1 s fill starts loading on the same map; cancelling loading returns to the plaza. Stands and the gallery now replace the difficulty and hero-select screens. Warm-up at 1440 is measured with the first styled frame, including GPU completion.
5. **`swapHero` (built):** tests that a swap during walk, windup, channel, catch, dash and a shot in flight keeps the id and position, carries clamped velocity and leaves nothing armed.
6. **Stands (built):** one cardboard cutout per `heroes.js` entry along the near edge, with a walk-on pad and picked marker. Entering a playable pad calls `swapHero`; locked silhouettes say "soon", wobble and bump you off without changing your pick. H and d-pad up cycle playable picks in place with the same plain cut. The current pick survives Back, loading cancellation and Start/Enter into the match. Clicks on a pad mean walk, even if a dummy's projected torso overlaps it. Denial queues Stop before the sim step so the old walk cannot carry you straight back onto the locked pad. Portrait widens the plaza FOV to keep the full row and all recovery marks visible; the flip waits for slice 10.
7. **Gallery (built):** three cardboard targets behind the hero stands, outside the dummy practice lanes; `pick` facts carry hero, cast token, ability, difficulty, point and tick. The original aim must be within 2 m and a real clipped footprint must touch the target. Vault landing and a basic pick; Rain over Normal and Hard picks the nearer even with reversed enumeration; exact ties use id; practice aimed at either dummy does not pick. Toss, Catch, Dive and Slap also pick, once per cast. G and d-pad down fire the next choice from a clear mark, with inherited/held presses ignored. The live gallery choice overrides the incoming difficulty through loading, cancel and match hand-off. The narrower court and closer stands make the hero readable at 390 while all walkable ground and marks remain in frame. The gallery is the difficulty picker; there is no separate selection screen.
8. **Ready line (built):** two chalk lines and six boxes in the compact court, five hero cardboard bots and an interpolated gold fill for the human. A fresh `/` browser trace manually walks into the box, steps out at 0.35 fill and empties it, then holds for 60 ticks to start loading. Loading cancellation keeps Mitts and Normal; Enter auto-walk is interrupted by RMB and Esc, then a fresh Enter holds for 60 ticks and starts the live match with those picks. Pad proof covers inherited Start, fresh Start, B cancellation and A on the nearby prompt. All six boxes and five standees fit at 390 and 1440. The DOM-free seat proof checks contested ticks and join-order ties in both call orders, bot replacement, unseated humans blocking Ready, and the every-human gate. No timers or Ready state live in module globals. The post-slice-9 proof uses Ready/cancel intents with two real humans, checks the detached six-seat snapshot and walks into another team's box.
9. **Inspection and entrance (built):** a fresh `/` trace goes straight from MOBA to the plaza, inspects a hero stand and a ready standee with hover/Alt, and inspects the aimed stand with Y. N opens the current hero's sheet; tick and both dummy positions stay frozen. B closes it without leaving, View opens/closes it, and arrows/stick scroll. A switched Mitts sheet shows Toss, Catch and Dive rather than Fletcher's kit. Esc with nothing held returns to the splash. `?mode=moba&play&hero=mitts&bots=hard` still enters the live six-hero match directly. The old difficulty/hero screens, kit card, portrait-only dressing and preview, selection backdrop art and unused CSS are removed. `front/stats.js` remains for the sheet's derived timing values. Screenshots cover 390, 1440 and 2560.
10. **Feel:** the flip, squash and overshoot, sounds. Screenshots at 390, 1440 and 2560 × 1080 from a fresh `/`.
11. **Online seats:** folded into [M6 build slices](moba-plan.md#build-slices) 1–4.
