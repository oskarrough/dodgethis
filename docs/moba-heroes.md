# MOBA heroes

The next three heroes after the Fletcher, for [moba-plan.md](moba-plan.md). Mitts comes from [moba-ideas.md](moba-ideas.md); Carom and Skip are new. Each one takes a dodgeball rule the Fletcher doesn't use: catch, ricochet, out-not-gone. Slice 1 is built: plugin-local definitions, Fletcher's retuned kit and all four silhouettes. Mitts is playable in practice with Pocket, Toss, Catch and Dive. Carom and Skip remain art-only stand-ins. R abilities wait for M5, like Volley.

**Dodge deadline.** The latest tick a centred target at 8 m can start moving and still clear, measured in the stepper (projectile radius, edge contact and tick order included), not derived by formula. The host needs 0.2 s of it; at 100 ms RTT a guest loses 0.12 s on average and 0.21 s at worst. So every dodgeable shot here has a deadline of at least 0.45 s, and most of it is windup that shows the hitbox. Measured on this design: Toss 0.55, Bank 0.60, Trick Shot 0.67, Pass 0.48, touchline throw 0.82 s.

**Fletcher's Q** takes the reviewer's fix: cast point 0.3 s with a ground line tell for enemies, and 20 m/s instead of 24. That moves its deadline from about 0.27 to 0.52 s.

**Tells.** Anything that damages, stops or moves an enemy has a cast point of at least 0.3 s and shows its exact hitbox to enemies for all of it. A caught shot sent back shows its line for at least 0.5 s. Self-only casts (stances, dashes) are instant and show their state on the ground while it lasts.

**Interception.** One moba-local resolver handles every catch. It only takes straight hero skillshots: never basics, minion or tower shots, which home. A caught shot is frozen as `{ damage, speed, radius, range, pierce }`, with damage including any cushions it had banked, and it comes back as a new straight shot owned by the catcher: no further banking, no trait procs for its original owner. It hits structures like any ability (25%, blocked when invulnerable). The Ball is never a shot here; see Mitts.

**Silhouettes.** The foot disc is the hitbox: every hero keeps the 0.45 m collision circle, drawn as an inked disc. Bodies above it may be slimmer or wider; the disc is the promise, the body is character. Each hero owns one body shape and one prop, and reuses no minion prop (shield disc, stick, cone hat, block helmet). Team colour sits on the disc, the prop and the top of the body, so a mirror match is one shape in two colours.

| Fletcher | ranged poke       | small circle: the capsule, slimmed       | quiver of three big fletched arrows, fanned behind him          |

## Slice 1: hero definitions

Before any new kit, moba has a hero table, inside the plugin and not core. Each entry holds base HP, speed, basic attack, one ability per slot and trait hooks. Level-ups scale the hero's own base, not `tune.hero`. Momentum becomes the Fletcher's trait hook, so another hero's Q doesn't recharge Vault. Piercing, healing, bouncing and catching become ability properties instead of branches on `slot1`. Pocket and bag are per-hero state, replicated and cleared on death. Boards and cutouts are sim-level lists; a cutout can exist while its owner is dead. Freeze is a timer on the frozen unit, next to its slow. Test: a match of six Fletchers built from the table matches today's full-lane test tick for tick. Screenshot all four silhouettes in grayscale at the lane's 58° pitch and one shared scale, with Fletcher at 20 px; the shape alone must tell them apart. Bodies sit on the disc; the quiver tilts back 35° and has flat fins. Mitts has an inked body edge and larger finger bumps, and Skip's bar is 1.3 m wide. Practice uses your selected hero (or `hero=mitts`) plus two allied Fletcher bots versus three enemy Fletcher bots. `heroes.js` exposes `playable` when a definition has a basic and a kit; Carom and Skip remain locked. `bots.js` reads the seat's definition for basics, abilities, ranges and damage. Mitts bots time Catch against enemy windups and shots in the lagged view, including the Ball. The pre-table equivalence fixture pins its original siege tuning so M4's deliberate balance changes do not rewrite that historical proof.

`heroes.js` owns the table; seats use `heroId` (default `fletcher`). Mitts has her own basic, kit and Pocket hook; Carom and Skip have their own base HP/speed but no basic, abilities or traits. `hero-view.js` dresses both the lane and hero select's replica. `abilityState` carries only pocket and bag. The snapshot carries complete projectile records (including frozen stats and `passed`), zones with ability identity, boards, cutouts, per-unit `{ until, factor }` slows and `freezeUntil`. The six-Fletcher proof hashes every tick's full pre-table snapshot and facts for 12 minutes against `b5f7e7b` with the Q retune applied. One bot per team also casts Vault and Rain through intents: the fixture records 95 Vaults, 49 Rains and 38 effective Momentum reductions. New replication fields and ability ids are excluded from the legacy digest, and tested separately. This is equivalence, not a six-scripted-bot win claim.

The sim owns the catch resolver. `openCatch(hero, { duration, radius, angle, acceptBall, mode, limit, resetSlot, resetCooldown })` arms a window; an enemy straight hero shot freezes into Pocket or Bag at the first eligible contact. Cover and boards win earlier contacts. A shot already touching the hero cannot be caught from behind after dealing damage. `returnsPocket: true` on a shot ability lets its press bypass cooldown and start the shared 0.5 s return tell; `throwCaught` exposes the same action. Stop or cancel aborts a windup; repeated RMB orders do not. Cancelling a return restores Pocket with its original expiry, never a fresh six seconds. `launchShot(hero, dir, stats, origin?)` launches a new owned shot with finite damage/speed/radius/range; a cutout can supply its inside-the-wall origin. Returned shots cannot bank or proc the original owner's trait. Only `acceptBall` windows take the Ball, into carried state with its lifetime unchanged and Pocket emptied.

Abilities identify their `tell`, `held` shape and `effects`, independent of their slot. Stance and channel kinds are mobile by default; a channel can declare `root` or `cancelOnMove`. `onStart`, `onTick`, `onRelease`, `onEnd`, `onCancel` and `onDashEnd` handle their own state transitions. Trait hooks receive `{ hero, sim, tick, ticks, intents, present }`: `onTick` also runs while dead, `onDeath` runs after clearing Pocket/Bag, `onCatch` receives the intercepted source and window (Pocket keeps the thrower's team outside its frozen shot), and `onHit` additionally receives source, shot and target. A death hook can create a cutout; respawn removes it. Prone and freeze block the affected hero's actions, while a Dive window can remain active through prone. These are mechanisms, not the three new kits.

## Mitts, the keeper

Frontline counter to skillshots. She stands in front of her team, takes shots and sends them back. Weak to Rain, basics and flanks. 1600 HP, 5.0 m/s. Basic: a glove slap, 3 m range, 110 damage, 1/s.

| Key   | Name       | Numbers                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trait | Pocket     | Holds one caught shot for 6 s. The glove glows the thrower's colour and a ring on her disc counts down; at zero it's gone. A new catch replaces it.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Q     | Toss       | Line skillshot, first hit. Cast point 0.35, range 9, 20 m/s, radius 0.35, 120 damage, cooldown 5. With something pocketed, Q throws that instead, through the resolver, with a 0.5 s cast point and its line shown, ignoring Q's cooldown.                                                                                                                 |
| W     | Catch      | Instant stance: glove up, half speed, 1.0 s. The first enemy skillshot or Ball to enter a 100° cone within 2 m of her front is caught for no damage. Cooldown 10, reset to 3 on a catch. Enemies see the cream arc for the whole window.                                                                                                                   |
| E     | Dive       | Instant 4 m dash toward the aim over 0.25 s, then prone for 0.4 s: no moving, casting or attacking. Catches skillshots like W along the dive and the prone, in a full circle, but lets the Ball pass. Cooldown 12. Blocked by structures.                                                                                                                  |
| R     | Dodge This | Channel up to 1.5 s, rooted, turning 180°/s toward the aim, catching every enemy skillshot entering a 120° cone within 3 m, up to 6; the Ball passes. Release on R or at the end: a 0.5 s windup shows every return line, then the lot fires in an even 60° fan, range 12. An empty bag throws three Tosses. A stun or death empties the bag. Cooldown 70. |

**The Ball.** Only W catches it, and it goes straight into the normal carried state with its 45 s life still running: ×0.85 speed, any press throws it under Ball rules. Whatever was pocketed is lost.

**Timing.** Catch is a read, not a guarantee. She presses on the enemy's windup: 1.0 s covers a 0.5 s cast point, the flight to her arc and 0.21 s of guest lag, but pressing too early still expires first. A returned Volley has a deadline of about 0.48 s through Q or R. Test early and late guest presses at 100 ms RTT.

**The moment:** the enemy Fletcher looses Volley at her carry. Mitts dives across the line, catches it lying on her front, gets up and sends 320 damage back through all three of them.

**Counter-play:** throw a cheap shot into the arc to burn W, or wait it out. The cone faces one way, so flank her. Rain, basics and tower shots ignore the glove. The prone after Dive is 0.4 s of free hits. A Mitts walking in Catch freezes to Red light; standing in it, she's safe.

## Carom, the trick shot

Burst from odd angles. The Fletcher pokes you down the lane; Carom finishes you behind the pillar. Strongest in the plaza and near walls, weakest in the open road. He hits rubber balls with a squash racket. 1300 HP, the lowest, 5.0 m/s.

| Key   | Name       | Numbers                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----- | ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trait | Kiss       | Basic attack: 5 m range, 80 damage, 1/s, homing. It then bounces to the nearest other enemy within 3 m, heroes first, for 40.                                                                                                                                                     |
| Q     | Bank       | Line skillshot, first hit. Bounces off pillars, hedges, structures, boundary walls and his board, up to 2 cushions. Cast point 0.35, path length 10, 18 m/s, radius 0.35, 110 damage plus 55 per cushion before the hit, cooldown 5.                                              |
| W     | Kick off   | Instant 4 m dash over 0.2 s. Terrain and his board reflect it, and it keeps the remaining distance. Each reflection takes 1.5 s off the cooldown. Cooldown 6. Blocked by structures, like Vault.                                                                                  |
| E     | Backboard  | Cast point 0.3, range 8. A board 3 m wide and 1.5 m tall, square to the aim line, for 4 s. His Bank and Trick Shot cushion off it; every other projectile, both teams', stops on it with a blocked cue. The Ball drops, as at a hedge. No body: heroes walk through. Cooldown 10. |
| R     | Trick Shot | Cast point 0.6. Up to 6 cushions, path length 40, 26 m/s, radius 0.5, pierces heroes (each once), 200 damage plus 60 per cushion before the hit. Enemies see the whole path as a dotted red pool-cue guide for the cast point. Cooldown 60.                                       |

His own indicator for Q and R draws the full bounce path, so aiming at a wall is learnable in one game. The board is how he brings walls into the open road, and how he stops a Volley without catching it.

**The moment:** the Fletcher limps home on 400 HP behind the far pillar. The red dotted line zigzags across the plaza, everyone scatters, and after five cushions it finds him for 500.

**Counter-play:** fight him in the open road, where there's nothing to bank off. Every bank path is printed for its cast point, so step off the line. Walk through the board and shoot around its ends. Mitts catches his balls, frozen at the damage they'd banked. His only escape, Kick off, wants a wall nearby.

## Skip, the captain

Support. Keeps her team in the game by healing, swapping and calling people back, and makes enemies stand still. She shouts through a megaphone. 1450 HP, 5.0 m/s. Basic: a tennis ball, 5 m range, 70 damage, 1/s, homing.

| Key   | Name          | Numbers                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Trait | Out, not gone | While dead she's a flat grey cutout on her side's touchline, walking its full length at 5 m/s, untargetable. One action, a throw: cast point 0.35, range 14, 12 m/s, radius 0.35, 60 damage, cooldown 3, heroes only. Each hit takes 2 s off her death timer.                        |
| Q     | Pass          | Line skillshot that stops at the first hero, friend or foe, and passes minions. Cast point 0.3, range 10, 20 m/s, radius 0.4. An ally heals 180; an enemy takes 90 and a 25% slow for 1 s. Cooldown 5.                                                                               |
| W     | Tag out       | The ally hero nearest the aim within 7 m. Cast point 0.3: her hand goes up and enemies see a dashed tether. Then the two swap places instantly. The ally's stun and slow end; Skip takes 30% less damage for 2 s. A Ball carrier keeps the Ball. Cooldown 14.                        |
| E     | Red light     | Cast point 0.5: she turns her back, raises the megaphone, and enemies see a red 5 m ring filling. On resolve, every enemy hero inside moving faster than 0.5 m/s is frozen for 1.25 s in its running pose. Standing, casting and attacking are safe. Minions ignore it. Cooldown 12. |
| R     | Back in!      | Needs a dead ally. Channel 1 s behind a big "Back in!" speech bubble; a stun breaks it. The ally with the longest death timer runs on from the touchline and respawns at her side at 50% HP. Cooldown 100. Denied with the icon flash if nobody is dead.                             |

**The cutout** is its own moba-local unit, not the dead hero's body: the sim ignores a dead hero's intents, so the cutout reads them instead. Its throws launch from the inside edge of the boundary wall, since a shot born outside the map expires at once. Hedges, pillars and structures block them. They never call tower or minion help. It's the ideas doc's global rule, tried on one hero first.

**The moment:** an ally drops Rain on two enemies and Skip calls red light. Walk out and freeze inside the circle; stand still and take the Rain. Most people run, and the Rain lands on two statues.

**Counter-play:** stop. Red light is beaten by standing still, not by leaving: from the centre, 5 m is out of reach in 0.5 s, and a Vault ends long before the resolve. Body-block Pass, since it stops at the first hero. Burst the ally during Tag out's tether, because Skip lands where they stood. Stun the Back in! channel.

**Online:** Red light asks a guest to let go of the stick, and 0.5 s leaves 0.29 s at worst. Tag out is a teleport with no displacement state; the guest's 2 m snap covers it.
