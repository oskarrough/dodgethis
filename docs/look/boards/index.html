Four settings for Dodgethis

Each board is a different world under the same snappy toy brawler. All four keep the four built heroes (Fletcher, Mitts, Carom, Skip) and Overthrow and Flagfall legible, so none of them is a restart.

Images are small copies for reference, each linked to its source. Game shots belong to their studios; Bruegel, Hokusai, Hiroshige, McCay and the photos come from Wikimedia Commons.

Research only. Nothing in the repo was touched.
