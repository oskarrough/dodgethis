# Flagfall material textures

## Delivered files and preparation

All four delivery PNGs are 2048 × 2048, straight-down square texture swatches. The built-in generator returned 1254 × 1254 native images; delivery files are resampled, not native 2K detail.

- `flagfall-court-ground.png`: recommended court material, pale fine grit with subdued hairline cracks, sand and chalk ghosts. Use around 8 metres per repeat, with the court patch shader retained.
- `flagfall-court-painterly.png`: alternate court, smoother handling and fewer conspicuous cracks. Oskar supplied another shallow-water game reference and authorised keeping choices in the repo.
- `flagfall-water-detail.png`: neutral grey with a very faint violet tint, a scalar colour/modulation detail map for gentle scrolling over the water plate. This is not an RGB tangent-space normal map. Blend toward neutral before multiplying if additional darkening is unwanted.
- `flagfall-hedge-top.png`: continuous canopy texture for hedge tops only, muted green-teal leaves, sparse tiny violet flowers and soft ambient gaps.

Generation and seam repair used the built-in image generator. Original tiles were offset by half their width and height to expose wrap seams centrally; the central seams were repaired. Delivery preparation compressed values into quiet envelopes (court RGB 77–91%, water 68–79%, hedge 26–54%). Narrow opposite-edge strips were eased together to ensure exact wrapping (12 pixels for court and water, 64 pixels for hedge), with matched terminal rows and columns.

## Tile proof

Assembled each delivered texture into a 4096 × 4096 2×2 preview. Inspected full repeats and 512 × 512 crops around each seam intersection. Corrected the remaining hedge seam before delivery. Opposite edge pixels match exactly on both axes for all four tiles.

Measured greyscale sRGB value spans after preparation:

| Tile            | Minimum |  Mean | Maximum |
| --------------- | ------: | ----: | ------: |
| Court ground    |   0.796 | 0.871 |   0.906 |
| Court painterly |   0.792 | 0.869 |   0.910 |
| Water detail    |   0.682 | 0.709 |   0.784 |
| Hedge top       |   0.271 | 0.382 |   0.522 |

Native originals, drafts, repair outputs, preparation scripts and full 2×2 proofs are retained in the generation thread's storage. No game code changed.

## Seam repair prompts

### flagfall-court-ground

Use case: precise-object-edit. This image is an offset texture swatch with the original wrap boundaries moved to a cross in the middle. Repair the visible central vertical and horizontal seams so material flows uninterrupted over them. Repaint ONLY the central cross (about 10% width along each central axis), matching neighbouring material; preserve the rest of the texture, especially its outer edges, exactly. Do not draw a cross, lines, borders or new focal details. Keep the existing top-down orthographic square surface and colours. Make the whole tarmac surface extremely quiet: hairline cracks, fine grit and chalk ghosts should barely separate from pale cream-sand, no dark flecks or white sparkles. Keep total value range approximately 15%. No directional lighting, text, characters, UI, red, blue or gold. 2048 x 2048 PNG.

## Painterly court alternative

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless tileable production game surface texture, 2048 x 2048 PNG square. Perfect straight-down 90-degree orthographic view, uniform scale over the entire surface; no perspective, no horizon, no frame. This is ONLY a material swatch filling every pixel, not a scene, not a presentation board. All four opposite edges must match seamlessly: textures leaving right continue from left, textures leaving bottom continue from top. Pattern uniform across the swatch, no vignette, no centre focal feature, no obvious repeated grid or large landmarks. Flat evenly diffuse ambient lighting with no baked light direction, no cast shadows. Restrained polished painterly/stylized environment material inspired by the pale calm schoolyard floor and muted overgrowth in dodgethis's existing Overthrow concepts and Flagfall water plate. Ground textures serve readability, not attention. No text, numbers, characters, UI, watermarks, red, blue, gold, orange, black outlines, bright green or dramatic highlights.
Material: worn pale schoolyard tarmac lightly covered with sand that wind and rain have redistributed. Entire surface in pale cream-sand with fine quiet grit, very faint shallow sand drifts, isolated very short and barely visible hairline cracks, tiny scuffs, and a few almost vanished chalk smears/ghosts, NO readable court markings or big chalk circles. No dark asphalt holes, no pebbles standing up, no broad cracks crossing the tile, no memorable scuff or long brush stroke, no distinct patch that repeats obviously. About 8 metres per tile: most marks under 15 cm, fine grain smaller still. Maintain roughly 15% TOTAL normalized value range at most, including darkest crack and brightest grain: sRGB luminance 0.78–0.90, mean about 0.85. Hairline cracks pale dusty taupe barely darker than background. Sand subtle enough to merge at the match camera; floor must read as a single pale quiet surface from a distance. Flat albedo only, not strong bump relief. Faint cream and sand hues only.
Alternative inspired by Oskar's newest flooded game reference: softer broad painterly surface handling, simplified gently worn sand patches, a few very small blurred pebbly scuffs, almost invisible sand-washed tarmac. Less photorealistic gritty detail than the first version, deliberately suppressing isolated dark pits and bright grains. No paving grid, big stones, readable tiles, rim, islands or water drawn here. Extremely pale cream-sand with no more than 15% tonal range. Organic faint washed chalk traces dissolve into the surface. Seamless across opposite edges, small unmemorable variation suitable for repetition every 8 metres. This is only the uniform playable ground material.

Generated with the built-in image generator. Sources: docs/moba-look.md References and Concept art; docs/look/overthrow-{fight,lobby,side}.webp and flagfall-water.webp. Oskar authorised copies of the finished choices in docs/look/; working outputs and proof remain in thread storage.
