Six worlds. Same quick scrap.

Six directions for a fast 3v3 hero brawler. Each has a title screen, three heroes, a match from the MOBA camera, and a lobby you can walk around. Nothing is settled.

Finders KeepersLow TideUproarYardbirdsPotluckBad Company

01 / The current world, made unmistakable

Finders Keepers

Every lost ball, glove and playground oddment lands in a desert beyond the fence.

Pick sides, play for keeps, and hang out on the same battered court between fights.

Shapes & palette

A world of lost playground equipment over the fence: pale chalk court on torn tarmac mesa above a dusty violet desert, enormous faint schoolyard moon, chain-link fence and bucket-built sandcastles. Palette warm parchment, dusty lavender, muted sage, charcoal. Heroes are the objects themselves with tiny simple limbs, not humans holding equipment: Fletcher a tall cylindrical cream quiver with three arrows and a small bent bow, Mitts a squat oversized worn taupe catcher's glove whose fingers form the head, Carom an angular cream squash racket with tiny limbs and open oval mesh head. Neutral materials with team bibs only.

02 / Harbour crews settle it on the slipway

Low Tide

The tide goes out; rival dock crews take over the slipway for a quick scrap.

Throw, hook and ricochet through a harbour that works as your lobby as well as your arena.

Shapes & palette

A working fishing harbour, no pirates or tropical resort. Very chunky human dockworkers, rain capes, rubber boots, buoys, coiled rope, fish crates. Palette bone concrete, dark desaturated sea-green, fog-grey, charcoal, muted peach. Three heroes: a short broad woman dockhand in sage waterproof apron carrying an enormous cream oval lifebuoy for catches, a tall lean fisherman in charcoal sou'wester with curved pole and rope hook, a square-bodied deck mechanic in peach dungarees carrying a long flat boat paddle for ricochets. Huge readable headgear and equipment; faceted simple faces; team sash colour only.

03 / The show is a three-on-three fight

Uproar

Three actors a side, one stage, and absolutely no agreement about who gets the spotlight.

Masks, props and trapdoors turn a theatre into a brawler; backstage is where you pick your part.

Shapes & palette

A mischievous tiny touring theatre world made of painted plywood, cardboard flats and chunky simple masks. Not medieval fantasy or ornate circus. Palette pale mint painted floor, dark aubergine curtains, dusty mauve and chalk cream, ink black. Three heroes: a slim triangular masked acrobat in a plain diamond-pattern neutral unitard wielding two flat fan blades; a squat pear-shaped stagehand with oversized round cream comedy mask and padded cylinder mallet; a tall rectangular diva shaped like a folded paper costume with an oval half-black half-cream face mask carrying a megaphone. Team ribbons detachable. Faces are graphic mask shapes, geometry cheap.

04 / The roof belongs to whoever holds it

Yardbirds

Pigeons, gulls and crows turn city rooftops into their own fiercely contested neighbourhood.

Scrap over the shiny prize, then loaf at the rooftop clubhouse while the next flock gathers.

Shapes & palette

All heroes are actual small anthropomorphic city birds, no humans, no sports equipment characters. A flat graphic urban roofscape at early peach dawn, graphic feather blocks not detailed plumage. Palette pale concrete, desaturated petrol green rooftops, pigeon-grey, muted peach, charcoal. Three heroes: a stout barrel pigeon with tiny head and fan-tail, a tall lanky gull with long pointed beak and wing-boomerang silhouette, a triangular charcoal crow with angular wing cape and one shiny bottlecap shield. All bipedal birds with only basic scarves for team colours. Roof silhouettes and tiny bird eyes extremely simple low-poly.

05 / Kitchen scraps with a competitive streak

Potluck

A mushroom, a dumpling and an aubergine can still ruin your evening in under five minutes.

The prep counter is the arena; the kitchen between rounds is somewhere to poke, practise and play.

Shapes & palette

A late-night communal kitchen populated by lively ingredients, NOT human chefs, NOT grotesque food, no realistic food texture. Palette flour-white, desaturated celery green, ink aubergine, pale peach, charcoal. Three heroes: a broad squat cream dumpling with simple folded top and two little arms wielding a saucepan-lid catch shield, a slim tall aubergine with flat leaf crown and a skewer spear, a small round mushroom with wide flat taupe cap wielding a rectangular spatula paddle. Expressive two-dot faces, tiny feet. Keep neutral purple and sage ingredients apart from combat red/blue. Soft low-poly forms, no gloss.

06 / Office supplies have settled on violence

Bad Company

When everyone goes home, the stationery starts settling departmental grudges.

Staplers tank, scissors flank, and the water-cooler lobby is a place to play rather than a menu.

Shapes & palette

After-hours drab 1970s office comedy at stationery scale, NO computers-as-heroes, no futuristic neon, no cyberpunk. Palette pale recycled paper, sage-green desks, dusty lilac, charcoal, pale orange accents. Three heroes are living office supplies with tiny simple limbs: a squat charcoal stapler with a big hinged jaw and cuboid body, a tall slim pair of cream-handled scissors with long pointed closed metal-blade legs, a wide round taupe adhesive-tape dispenser with a visible circle hole and short projecting tape shield. Dot eyes restrained, detachable team paperclip bibs. A photocopier and huge swivel chair provide scale at edges.
