Oskar / 01

Sun Spell

Bright spaces, brighter mischief.

A caped star, a cloud and a leaf sprite scrap on grassy stone islands above warm pink clouds.

All four images follow Oskar’s Sun Spell panel. The floating pillars also inform the title, match and lobby.

Shapes & palette

Extruded star, puff spheres and leaf planes; tall rock prisms with grassy tile caps. Warm baked light, a quiet pencil-grain tile and simple waterfall ribbons.

Oskar / 02

Neon Dream

Quiet places, wild things.

Little luminous creatures settle quick fights on violet islands inside a sleeping flower jungle.

All four images follow Oskar’s Neon Dream panel.

Shapes & palette

Violet rock prisms, flat leaf fans and bell flowers; luminous dots and halo billboards at the perimeter. Inked pale creatures stay clear against a quiet violet floor.
